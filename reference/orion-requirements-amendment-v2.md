# Orion Equipment Leasing
## Equipment Lease Application — Requirements Amendment Pack V2

**Document reference:** ORN-AMD-002  
**Amends:** ORN-BR-001, Version 1.0  
**Document version:** 2.0  
**Recorded date:** 20 October 2026  
**Classification:** Fictional / Training Material

### 1. Purpose

Orion Equipment Leasing is introducing changes to its fictional equipment-lease application process to improve customer communication, introduce additional business controls, and refine operational deadlines.

This amendment pack supplements the original 42 business requirements in ORN-BR-001.

Unless expressly amended, the original requirements remain applicable.

Each amendment identifies its business-effective date and specifies whether it applies only to newly created applications or also to applications already in progress.

All organisations, applications, financial amounts, eligibility criteria and external services are fictional.

---

### 2. Amendments

### ORN-AMD-001 — Preliminary Offer Review

**Amends:** ORN-REQ-023, ORN-REQ-025, ORN-REQ-027  
**Effective date:** 1 January 2027  
**Applicability:** Applications created on or after 1 January 2027.

After an application receives approval but before a lease offer is released to the customer, an authorised Orion representative must complete a Preliminary Offer Review.

The review must confirm that the approved equipment, proposed lease duration and fictional pricing information remain consistent with the approval.

An application must not proceed to customer offer delivery until this review has been completed successfully.

Where material discrepancies are identified, the application must be referred for renewed business authorisation.

### ORN-AMD-002 — Revised Offer Validity Period

**Amends:** ORN-REQ-030, ORN-REQ-031, ORN-REQ-035  
**Effective date:** 1 February 2027  
**Applicability:** Offers issued on or after 1 February 2027, including offers relating to applications created earlier.

The validity period for newly issued offers is reduced from seven calendar days to five calendar days.

Offers issued before 1 February 2027 retain their originally recorded expiry date and time.

An existing outstanding offer must not have its expiry date changed solely because this amendment becomes effective.

### ORN-AMD-003 — Restrictions on Cancellation During Final Offer Preparation

**Amends:** ORN-REQ-039, ORN-REQ-041  
**Effective date:** 15 January 2027  
**Applicability:** All applications that have not yet entered final offer preparation on the effective date, including applications already in progress.

Once final offer preparation has begun, a customer cancellation request requires authorisation from an Orion reviewer before the application can be cancelled.

Requests received before final offer preparation begins remain subject to the original cancellation requirements.

A cancellation request awaiting authorisation must not itself be treated as confirmation that the application has been cancelled.

### ORN-AMD-004 — Terminology Change: Manual Review to Specialist Assessment

**Amends:** ORN-REQ-017, ORN-REQ-019, ORN-REQ-020, ORN-REQ-022  
**Effective date:** 1 January 2027  
**Applicability:** All new and in-progress applications.

The business term *Manual Review* is renamed *Specialist Assessment*.

The revised term describes the same business activity, authority requirements and responsibilities.

The terminology change does not, by itself, require additional assessment or invalidate decisions previously recorded using the former term.

Historical records may retain the terminology in use when the relevant activity occurred.

### ORN-AMD-005 — Revised Specialist Assessment Referral Threshold

**Amends:** ORN-REQ-017  
**Effective date:** 1 March 2027  
**Applicability:** Applications whose initial eligibility assessment begins on or after 1 March 2027.

The equipment-value threshold for mandatory Specialist Assessment is reduced from £100,000 to £80,000.

An application requesting equipment valued above £80,000 must be referred for Specialist Assessment even where its other eligibility information would otherwise permit an automatic decision.

Applications whose initial eligibility assessment was completed before the effective date retain the assessment outcome reached under the previously applicable requirements, unless a new assessment is independently required.

### ORN-AMD-006 — Independent Customer Confirmation Before Acceptance

**Amends:** ORN-REQ-031, ORN-REQ-032  
**Effective date:** 1 February 2027  
**Applicability:** Offers issued on or after 1 February 2027.

Before an offer acceptance becomes final, Orion must obtain a separate synthetic customer confirmation referencing the exact offer terms.

The confirmation must be received while the offer remains valid.

An acceptance request without the required confirmation must not result in a completed acceptance.

The confirmation requirement does not apply retrospectively to offers issued before the effective date.

### ORN-AMD-007 — New Customer Confirmation Service

**Amends:** ORN-REQ-032, ORN-REQ-042  
**Effective date:** 1 February 2027  
**Applicability:** Applications subject to ORN-AMD-006.

Orion must request a synthetic confirmation record from its fictional Customer Confirmation Service when the customer completes the additional offer-confirmation activity.

The service must return a confirmation reference that can be associated with the applicable offer and customer.

A temporary service failure must not be interpreted as successful confirmation.

Duplicate responses must not create multiple accepted confirmations for the same customer action.

The confirmation record must be retained with the associated application evidence.

### ORN-AMD-008 — Retirement of Separate Internal Offer-Decline Notification

**Amends:** ORN-REQ-034  
**Effective date:** 1 April 2027  
**Applicability:** Offer declines recorded on or after 1 April 2027.

Orion will no longer issue a separate internal notification solely to inform the equipment operations team that a customer has declined an offer.

The decline must still be recorded, and any required release of reserved equipment must still be arranged.

Material customer communications remain subject to ORN-REQ-042.

The removal of the separate internal notification does not remove the requirement to preserve evidence of the decline.

### ORN-AMD-009 — New Application Verification Category

**Amends:** ORN-REQ-002, ORN-REQ-006, ORN-REQ-008  
**Effective date:** 1 March 2027  
**Applicability:** Applications created on or after 1 March 2027 only.

New applications must include a synthetic Business Verification Category selected from Orion's approved fictional category list.

The category is mandatory before submission and must be included when determining applicable supporting-document requirements.

Applications created before 1 March 2027 are not required to supply this new field solely because the amendment becomes effective.

### ORN-AMD-010 — Additional Verification for In-Flight Applications

**Amends:** ORN-REQ-015, ORN-REQ-023, ORN-REQ-025  
**Effective date:** 15 February 2027  
**Applicability:** All applications that have not yet received final approval by the effective date, including applications submitted earlier.

Before final approval, Orion must confirm that the applicant's primary business contact details have been verified within the preceding 30 calendar days.

Applications awaiting assessment or Specialist Assessment on 15 February 2027 must meet this requirement before receiving final approval.

Applications already approved before that date do not require a new contact verification solely because of this amendment.

Failure to obtain verification must prevent final approval until the requirement is satisfied or the application reaches another permitted conclusion.

### ORN-AMD-011 — Revised Document-Request Follow-Up Period

**Amends:** ORN-REQ-011, ORN-REQ-012  
**Effective date:** 1 February 2027  
**Applicability:** Initial document requests issued on or after 1 February 2027.

Customers must provide outstanding requested documents within seven calendar days of the initial request, rather than ten.

The existing authorised extension of five calendar days remains available.

Document requests issued before the effective date retain their original deadline and any extension already granted.

The shorter deadline must not be applied retrospectively to an existing document request.

### ORN-AMD-012 — Revised Escalation Requirements

**Amends:** ORN-REQ-022  
**Effective date:** 1 March 2027  
**Applicability:** Specialist Assessments that have not received a final decision by the effective date.

An application undergoing Specialist Assessment must be escalated to a senior reviewer if it remains undecided for more than two business days after all mandatory evidence becomes available.

The previous escalation target was three business days.

For assessments already in progress on 1 March 2027, the new requirement applies to any remaining undecided assessment, with the original date on which all mandatory evidence became available remaining the reference point.

Escalation must not automatically change the eligibility result.

### ORN-AMD-013 — Retrospective Clarification of Offer Withdrawal

**Amends:** ORN-REQ-026, ORN-REQ-031, ORN-REQ-037  
**Recorded date:** 20 October 2026  
**Stated business-effective date:** 1 July 2026  
**Applicability:** Relevant offer-withdrawal circumstances occurring on or after 1 July 2026.

Orion has identified an ambiguity in its original requirements concerning customer responses to withdrawn offers.

The intended business requirement has always been that an offer withdrawn following a material change to approved equipment, equipment value or lease duration cannot subsequently be accepted, even if its original expiry date has not passed.

This amendment records that interpretation as applying from 1 July 2026.

The later recording date must remain distinguishable from the stated earlier business-effective date.

Previously completed decisions and customer communications must remain available for review; this clarification does not independently authorise an already concluded application to be reopened or silently changed.

### ORN-AMD-014 — Additional Approval for High-Value Offer Changes

**Amends:** ORN-REQ-020, ORN-REQ-023, ORN-REQ-026  
**Effective date:** 1 April 2027  
**Applicability:** Material changes proposed on or after 1 April 2027 for offers involving equipment valued above £75,000, including existing applications.

Where the approved equipment, lease duration or equipment value is materially changed after approval, a replacement offer may be authorised only after an additional senior-reviewer approval.

An ordinary reviewer approval alone is insufficient for these qualifying changes.

The additional authorisation must be recorded before a replacement offer is released.

Offers already accepted before the change is proposed are not automatically reopened.

### 3. General Interpretation

The following principles apply to this amendment pack.

**Original requirements:** ORN-BR-001 remains applicable except where explicitly amended.

**Effective dates:** An amendment's effective date identifies when its business requirement becomes applicable. It is distinct from the date the amendment was recorded.

**In-progress applications:** Applicability to existing applications is expressly stated for each amendment. No amendment should be presumed to apply retrospectively to every application.

**Historical evidence:** Records of assessments, customer decisions, document requests, communications and authorisations must retain sufficient context to explain the requirements applicable when they occurred.

**Outstanding activities:** Where an amendment affects an application with outstanding document requests, customer communications or external-service operations, the applicable amendment must be determined from its expressly stated business conditions.

**Final outcomes:** These amendments do not authorise automatic reopening of permanently rejected applications or other concluded applications where the original requirements prohibit reopening.

**Synthetic environment:** All business rules, dates, entities and services described here are fictional and intended for a state-machine engineering exercise.
